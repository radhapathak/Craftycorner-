About Crafty Corner

Crafty Corner is a handmade brand where each piece is made with care and personal attention. Every order is discussed directly with you, so your gift or favour feels truly yours.

How to Order

  1. Choose
    Pick a product, option and quantity.
  2. Send on WhatsApp
    Tap “Order on WhatsApp” to send a prefilled message.
  3. Confirm
    We confirm availability, price, personalisation and dispatch time with you.
  4. Pay & receive
    Payment method and delivery are agreed on WhatsApp.

FAQ & Policies

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and tap Order on WhatsApp. Your order is only confirmed once we reply and agree details with you.

Can products be personalised?

Many can. Share your requirements on WhatsApp and we will confirm what is possible.

Delivery

Dispatch time and delivery charges depend on the product, quantity and city, and are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Cancellation & returns

Personalised items are usually non-returnable. Please message us on WhatsApp about any cancellation or return before you place your order.

Bulk and wedding orders

Please enquire as early as possible with your event date and quantity so we can confirm feasibility.
