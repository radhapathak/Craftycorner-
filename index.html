about us

Small batches, made by hand

How we make it

Every candle and bouquet is made at our table, by hand.

Craftycorner makes handmade things in small batches: soy wax candles set with dried petals, everlasting flowers, jewellery, wedding pieces and pipe cleaner crafts.

Every piece is made by hand, so no two are exactly the same. We would rather make a few things carefully than many things in a hurry.

What we make

  • Candles: soy wax, wooden wicks, real dried petals.
  • Flowers: everlasting blooms and bouquets that keep their colour.
  • Jewellery: resin, flower and pearl pieces, including bridal and wedding pieces made to your colours.
  • Pipe cleaner crafts: handmade decor and gifts.
